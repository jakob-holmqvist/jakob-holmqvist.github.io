INDUSTRIAL IMPRINTS (ARTIFICIAL LANDSCAPES)

performance at Prøvestenen industrial imprints  in collaboration with lasse folke - http://lassefolke.dk An artistic investigation of the 'Anthropocene' with lasse gam folke. Materials: animal distemper, iron oxide red and black pigment, natural cotton textiles, steel print machine, douglas fir (leftover from industrial wooden planks)  'industrial imprints' august (2024)  at Prøvestenen / ikke aaben in collaboration with lasse gam folke  . a huge thank you to all the kind sponsors supported by Beckett Fonden Dinesen Dreyers Fond Grosserer L.F.
