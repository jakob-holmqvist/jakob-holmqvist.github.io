HEDENSTED LIBRARY

permanent public sculpture open book / traces in time in collaboration with lasse folke - http://lassefolke.dk open book/traces in time. as an interplay between art and architecture the glass books cover the gable of Hedensted library. the glass books resemble a window into the text universe of the library hall. full of text and traces of writers from the past to the present.  local involvement. kids and adults from Hedensted were invited to write text bits about 'what writing meant to them' to underline and familiarise the immediate approach to writing and the myriad of thoughts that it invokes. it also touches upon the systematics behind reading and writing - and the immense significance that it has in our society. what if you can't read? the sun plays with the monochrome letters and aluminium panels to either produce or diffuse meaning.   'open book / traces in time' permanent sculpture (2022)  for Hedensted Kommune + 17gavle.dk in collaboration with lasse gam folke  . engineer  jeppe steen andersen - Eduard troelsgård rådgivende ingeniører . glass  Glasekperten.dk . steel  PL Kleinsmedie  . supported by den jyske kunstfond region midtjylland 17gavle.dk hedensted kommune hedensted handels-standsforening . photos by  jakob holmqvist larsen  and lasse folke Proces sketches - visualisations - local involvement - construction kan man læse, hvis man er ordblind? text bits from the kids workshop with hedensted school construction
